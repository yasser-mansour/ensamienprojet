# Nine presenter role assignments

New documentation plan based on recovered sources; P1 captain, P9 technical co-pilot.

## P1 - Team captain / mission commander

Opening, crash alert, final synthesis; protect teaching and approve fallback decisions.

P8 handles the room; P3 sends a two-minute warning. Do not troubleshoot phones while narrating.

Zone: No fixed zone: captain at front

Preparation: Rehearse the ball demonstration safely, the 33-34 narrative bridge, and a 60-second closing. Check all nine attendance and handoff readiness.

## P2 - Energy engineer

Teach energy; lead Mission 01; explain physical examples.

After your mission, mentor Alpha; help P5 explain force reduction and review one design pitch.

Zone: Alpha

Preparation: Rehearse each of the five situations, including the ramp sequence; explain potential energy as position/configuration rather than just being still.

## P3 - Dynamics engineer / timekeeper

Teach slides 21-26; track the segment clock; collect mentor scores.

Keep the master clock throughout. Mentor Bravo, record offline EP, and cover host clicks only for the final mission.

Zone: Bravo

Preparation: Practise decelerate, stopping distance, force and work examples. Practise host CLOSE/REVEAL/NEXT for the final under P9 supervision.

## P4 - Research engineer

Teach slides 27-30; lead Mission 02; explain matching and vocabulary.

Mentor Charlie after vocabulary; prepare P7 Task 7 gap answers and read Engineer 1 if audio fails.

Zone: Charlie

Preparation: Learn Task 4 matches (1-A, 2-D, 3-C, 4-B). Rehearse the actual app matching question; compare it with the local quiz bank without changing application content.

## P5 - Crash investigator

Lead reading and Mission 03; distinguish quoted text from supplementary science.

Mentor Delta; supply science clarification during reading; review design reasoning with P7.

Zone: Delta

Preparation: Read slides 35-40 and all seven statements. Separate literal reading answers from standard-physics clarification. Rehearse a three-minute reading window.

## P6 - Communication engineer

Teach five language patterns; lead Mission 04; validate oral repairs.

Mentor Echo; read Engineer 2 with P4 if audio fails; check comparatives/future forms in pitches and reports.

Zone: Echo

Preparation: Explain can + base verb, comparative -er, be going to, future present continuous and passive. Practise five fixes and the Engineer 2 dialogue.

## P7 - Testing and speaking engineer

Lead listening and pitches; manage two listening passes and 45-second presentations.

Mentor Foxtrot outside your teaching block; announce pitch speakers; keep audio and speaking within eight minutes each.

Zone: Foxtrot

Preparation: Test available official audio. Prepare the live dialogue with P4/P6; leave gap 5 ungraded unless verified. Practise selecting two pitches after four minutes of preparation.

## P8 - Writing engineer / floor coordinator

Lead report task; distribute materials; coordinate zone mentors and participation.

Coordinate all eight companies; directly mentor Golf and Hotel with rotating help from the idle teaching presenters. Collect report drafts and log participation evidence.

Zone: Golf and Hotel

Preparation: Count 90 handouts (or 45 shared copies), make eight company signs, and prepare an EP ledger. Rehearse a four-minute report-start task rather than demanding a completed 120-word report.

## P9 - Technical co-pilot / final simulation lead

Operate host and projector; lead final sequence explanation while P3 briefly operates the host.

Monitor connectivity and projected state; execute the five host missions; keep offline score totals separate. Teach final cards at 102 minutes while P3 controls the host.

Zone: No fixed zone: laptop station

Preparation: Rehearse host state transitions, PAUSE/RESUME, projector reopen, recovery code, manual adjustment with reason, and final B-D-A-C. Verify deployed content against this source snapshot before class.
