- A. It disappears completely

## LANGUAGE

### L1
**"The new seatbelt is ______ than the current one."**
- A. safe
- B. **safer** ✅
- C. more safe
- D. most safer

**Explanation:** Short adjectives take *-er* (never "more" + *-er*).
**Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** supplementary (built on the Task 8 language box)

### L2
**"______ you going to make the seatbelts stretch more?"**
- A. Do
- B. **Are** ✅
- C. Will
- D. Is

**Explanation:** *be going to* = SUBJECT + BE + GOING TO + BASE VERB. Quoted from Task 8's language box.
**Difficulty:** easy · **Source:** textbook

### L3
**"We ______ it next week." (a fixed, scheduled arrangement)**
- A. test
- B. tested
- C. **are testing** ✅
- D. will tested

**Explanation:** Present continuous (be + verb-ing) for a future arrangement. From Task 7's dialogue: "We're
testing it next week."
**Difficulty:** medium · **Source:** textbook

### L4
**Choose the correct passive: "Energy ______ to the belt."**
- A. will transfer
- B. will transferred
- C. **will be transferred** ✅
- D. is transfer

**Explanation:** Future passive = WILL + BE + PAST PARTICIPLE. From Task 7: "…will be transferred to the belt."
**Difficulty:** challenge · **Source:** textbook

## FINAL REVISION

### FR1
**Put in order: which of these happens FIRST in the crash sequence?**
- A. Injuries decrease
- B. **Seatbelt stretches** ✅
- C. Force on the passenger decreases
- D. Stopping distance increases

**Explanation:** The causal chain is: seatbelt stretches → stopping distance increases → force/Gs decrease →
injuries decrease.
**Difficulty:** challenge · **Source:** supplementary (built on the Abstract's own causal chain)

### FR2
**With non-stretching seatbelts, the passenger's stopping distance is…**
- A. long
- B. **short** ✅

**Explanation:** "With non-stretching seatbelts, the passenger's stopping distance is short." (Background)

### FR3

- A. a summary of a document
- B. to move from one object to another
- C. **the unit of force that equals the force of gravity** ✅
- D. the idea that a moving object's energy equals the distance it moves

**Explanation:** Textbook Task 4, matching item C = G.
**Difficulty:** medium · **Source:** textbook

### FR4
**Which sentence correctly repairs this broken sentence: "We can to make it safer."**
